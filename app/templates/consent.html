Before you continue

To use your firm workspace, please review and accept how we handle data (aligned with the DPDP Act, 2023):

  • Your client & matter data is used only to serve your firm.
  • It is never used to train AI models, and never shared across firms.
  • AI output is advisory — a human advocate reviews everything before filing.
  • You can export or delete your data at any time (Account → Data rights).
