Billing questions

Is there a free trial?

Yes — 14 days of the Solo Advocate or Firm plan, full features, no credit card required. We remind you before it ends. Nothing is charged automatically without your consent.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, in two clicks from Account → Billing. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you've already paid for. No cancellation fees, no phone call required.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

Your data is retained for 30 days so you can reactivate, then you can export or permanently delete it anytime. We follow DPDP data-rights rules.

Do you offer refunds?

Annual plans are refundable on a pro-rata basis within the first 14 days.

Is GST included?

Prices are shown exclusive of GST; applicable GST is added at checkout. Firms can add their GSTIN to receive a tax invoice for input credit.

Do you train AI on my data?

No. Your matter and client data are never used to train models. This is enforced in the product, not just promised here.

Is this legal advice?

No. Juriscite assists advocates; every AI output must be reviewed by a qualified advocate before use or filing.

Juriscite is a practice tool for advocates and law firms, not a public legal-advice service or a lawyer marketplace. AI outputs require advocate review before filing.
