# AI Use & Disclosure Policy — LegalServer.AI

**Version:** 0.1 (DRAFT) · **Last updated:** 2026-06-23
Aligns with the SC draft "Regulations for Use of AI in Courts, 2026" (human primacy, transparency).

## How AI is used
- **Cited legal research:** the AI retrieves real statute/judgment text from a defined corpus and
  answers **only** from what it retrieved. **No source → no answer.** Every legal claim carries a citation.
- **Drafting:** the AI generates document drafts from templates. Every draft starts as
  `DRAFT_FOR_ADVOCATE_REVIEW` and is **not final** until a qualified advocate approves it.

## Human primacy
AI is **advisory**. A qualified advocate holds authority and responsibility for every output. The app
never represents AI output as a final legal opinion, filing, or court decision.

## Transparency / disclosure
- AI-generated content is labelled as such in the app and on exports.
- Confidence labels (HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW) accompany research answers.
- If asked to hide its AI nature from a court, the AI refuses.

## Prohibited AI uses
Case-outcome prediction, win-probability, bail/recidivism risk-scoring, witness-credibility scoring,
judge-behaviour prediction, automated filing, AI evidence-authenticity assertions, and outcome
guarantees are **permanently disabled** (see PROHIBITED_FEATURES.md).

## No training on your data
Client/matter data is never used to train or fine-tune AI models (see AI_DATA_POLICY.md).
