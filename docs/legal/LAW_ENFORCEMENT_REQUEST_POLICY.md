# Law-Enforcement / Court-Order Request Policy — LegalServer.AI

**Version:** 0.1 (DRAFT) · **Last updated:** 2026-06-23

How we handle requests from police, courts, or government for user/tenant data.

## Principles
- **No disclosure without Founder + legal approval**, except a narrowly-defined emergency process
  defined by counsel (**[MISSING / TO BE POPULATED]**).
- **Minimum necessary:** disclose only the specific data lawfully required.
- **Validate** the legal authority/validity of every request before responding.
- **Audit** every request and disclosure in the Legal Request register.
- **Notify** the affected user where lawfully permitted.

## Process
1. Receive request; log it in the Legal Request register (source, type, date, affected tenant/user,
   order attached, response deadline).
2. Validate authenticity and legal validity.
3. Founder + counsel approve scope.
4. Prepare a minimum-necessary export package.
5. Respond within the lawful deadline; record what was disclosed and the approver.
6. Close and review.
