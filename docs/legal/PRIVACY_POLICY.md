## 2. Data we collect
- **Account:** name, email, phone, password (hashed), role, professional ID (if provided).
- **Firm/tenant:** firm name, members, roles.
- **Matter data you enter:** client names/contacts, matter facts, documents/uploads, court/hearing
  data, fees/billing data, tasks, drafts.
- **AI usage:** prompts and outputs you generate.
- **Operational:** audit logs, IP address, device/browser metadata, diagnostic logs.
- (Payment metadata and support tickets if/when those features are enabled.)

## 3. Why we process it (purposes)
Account authentication; firm workspace; matter/client management; document storage & versioning;
cited legal research and drafting; reminders/notifications; security, audit and support; billing if
enabled. We process the minimum necessary for these purposes.

## 4. Lawful basis & consent
Processing is based on your consent (captured at registration and on policy updates) and the
performance of our service to you. Consent is versioned and recorded (see Consent receipts).

## 5. No model training on your data
**Client and matter data is never used to train or fine-tune AI models, and is never shared across
firms.** AI calls are routed to a provider only to produce your requested output.

## 6. Sharing & subprocessors
We do not sell your data. We use the subprocessors listed in the Subprocessor Register (e.g. cloud
hosting, the AI model provider) strictly to deliver the service.

## 7. Retention
See the Retention Policy. Matter data is retained for the advocate's needs (limitation periods) until
you delete it or request erasure; transient data (e.g. read notifications) is auto-purged.

## 8. Your rights (DPDP)
Access/export, correction, erasure (subject to lawful retention), and grievance redressal. Exercise
these in **Account → Privacy & Data Rights**, or contact the Grievance Officer.

## 9. Security
Tenant isolation, role-based access, audit logging, field encryption at rest for sensitive secrets,
HTTP security headers, backups. Storage-level encryption and full security review are pre-real-data gates.
