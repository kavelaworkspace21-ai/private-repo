# Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) — LegalServer.AI

**Version:** 0.1 (DRAFT) · **Last updated:** 2026-06-23

You may use LegalServer.AI only for lawful legal-practice purposes, as an advocate or authorised firm
member. The following are **prohibited**:

## Prohibited content & conduct
- Uploading documents or data you are **not authorised** to upload for that firm/matter.
- Defamatory, harassing, threatening, obscene or impersonating content.
- Privacy violations or exposing third parties' personal data without lawful basis.
- Infringing copyright or other IP.
- Malware, exploits, or attempts to breach security / other tenants' data.
- Scraping, CAPTCHA-bypass, or unauthorised access to eCourts or any court system.

## Prohibited AI misuse
- Generating a **fake/false affidavit, complaint, or evidence**.
- Using AI output as a **final filing without advocate review**.
- Seeking **guaranteed outcomes, win-probability, case/bail/risk prediction**, or other prohibited
  features (these are blocked; see PROHIBITED_FEATURES.md).
- Attempting to make the AI **hide its AI-generated nature** from a court.
- Prompt-injection or attempts to extract another firm's data.

## Prohibited positioning
- Using the platform to **advertise/solicit** legal work or generate client leads (BCI Rule 36).
- Presenting the platform as **public legal advice**.

## Consequences
Violations may lead to content restriction, account/tenant suspension, evidence preservation, and
referral to authorities where required. Report misuse via the in-app **Report misuse** flow or the
Grievance Officer.
